   - College dropdown with 5 options for students and mentors

   - Mentors can schedule sessions for students
   - Go to "My Students" → Click "Schedule Session"
   - Select student, date, time, topic, and duration

   - Unread notification count shown on bell icon badge
   - Can delete notifications individually

✅ Theme Toggle:
   - Dark/Light mode available on all pages
   - Settings persist across sessions

✅ Tab Navigation:
   - Click same tab twice shows: "You are already in the [Tab] tab"
   - Prevents unnecessary page reloads
